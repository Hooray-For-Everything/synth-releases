# Feedback

Use this public repository's Issues tab for instrument feedback. Include the instrument and exact version, Mac architecture, macOS version, host/version, and short steps to reproduce the problem. Say what you expected and what happened.

Issues and attachments here are public. Share a small, intentional example rather than a private song/session, personal recording, account detail or full log containing home paths. Existing private conversations are not copied into this tracker.

These are early releases and playable previews. Full host behavior, real-time performance and listening acceptance remain incomplete; availability does not promise production stability. Preserve important projects while evaluating them.
